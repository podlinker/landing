Following a podcast doesn't mean someone has listened to or recommended every episode.

You don't follow topics. You follow people.

Each profile is a personal, varied collection of podcasts. Follow someone and new episodes from the podcasts they follow enter your feed, ordered by publication date.

One episode, once.

If two people in your network follow the same podcast, each episode appears only once. You can see whose collection it belongs to without mistaking that for a listen or a recommendation.
